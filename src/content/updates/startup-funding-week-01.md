With the Expert System behind us, we started Challenge 02, which brings together Aprendizagem Automática and Planeamento e Tomada de Decisão. This first week was mostly about choosing the theme and starting to look for data.

## The theme

We chose startup funding. Startups raise money in rounds, and most of them never reach the next one. We want to use funding-round data to estimate which startups are likely to succeed, and then use those estimates to decide how to invest when capital is limited.

## Looking for data

We started researching datasets. The best-known sources, such as Crunchbase and Dealroom, are paid, and many of the free ones are old or synthetic. We want data that is free, real and recent, so the search continues.

## Next week

We will keep researching datasets and choose the ones to work with.
